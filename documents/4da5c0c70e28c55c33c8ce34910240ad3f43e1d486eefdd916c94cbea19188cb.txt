Embeddings are dense numerical vectors that represent the meaning or characteristics of text. A sentence embedding maps a sentence or passage to a fixed-length vector. Semantically related texts should have vectors that are close according to a similarity measure. Cosine similarity compares vector direction and ranges from minus one to one mathematically, with larger values indicating more similar directions. Inner product is a dot product; when both vectors are normalized to unit length, inner product equals cosine similarity. Euclidean or L2 distance measures geometric distance, so smaller values mean closer vectors. Sentence Transformers are bi-encoder models that encode queries and documents independently, which makes retrieval efficient because document vectors can be precomputed. Dense retrieval can match synonyms and related concepts even when the exact query words are absent. Embedding quality depends on the model and domain. Vector normalization is important when cosine similarity is implemented using an inner-product index. A vector index stores embeddings and supports nearest-neighbor search.
