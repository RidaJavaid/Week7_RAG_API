FAISS is a library for efficient similarity search over dense vectors. IndexFlatL2 performs exact search using squared Euclidean distance, where lower distance is better. IndexFlatIP performs exact maximum inner-product search, where higher score is better. Inner product is not automatically cosine similarity. To perform cosine similarity with an inner-product FAISS index, normalize both database vectors and query vectors to unit L2 length before indexing and searching. For normalized vectors, squared L2 distance and inner product are monotonically related, so they produce equivalent rankings. Flat indexes are exhaustive and useful for small corpora and correctness checks. Approximate indexes such as IVF or HNSW trade some recall for speed and scalability on larger collections. Nearest-neighbor search returns vector identifiers that must be mapped back to chunk text and source metadata.
